**Make the most of your points and miles.**

Miley is an iPhone app for Indian credit card rewards and travel loyalty programs. It helps you put the points you already have to good use, and then tells you which card to pay with next.

- **Use your points.** See what your points are worth and where they can go, including multi-step transfers from a bank's rewards to airline and hotel programs.
- **Pick the right card.** Ask which of your cards earns the most for a purchase, with caps and exclusions taken into account.
- **Know what each card offers.** Earn rates, redemption options and benefits for each card you hold.

## Private by design

- No sign-in and no account.
- No backend and no tracking.
- Your cards and balances stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
- The only thing Miley downloads is a public card catalog, which is signed and checked before the app uses it.

Point values are estimates unless a program fixes them, and Miley labels which is which.

## Status

Miley is in development and not yet on the App Store.
